A FAIRER TEST

Keep samples together.
Trust the test.

Images from the same wafer can look alike. If one crop enters training and another enters testing, a model may recognize the sample instead of learning defects.

When sample IDs exist, WaferScope assigns whole sample folders to one split. Carinthia’s CSV has no wafer/sample ID, so its run uses an image-level split and cannot measure performance on unseen wafers.

FNR = FN / (FN + TP) share of actual defects missed
